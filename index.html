About BioGC

The Laboratory of Bioinformatics and Clinical Genomics (BioGC) is a center of excellence at the Carlos Chagas Institute – Fiocruz Paraná.

Mission: Generate knowledge and innovation in bioinformatics and clinical genomics, contributing to the advancement of science and public health, in line with SUS principles.

Translational research

Integrating omics data into clinical practice.

Research lines

Pathogen genomics

Genomic surveillance and antimicrobial resistance.

Transcriptomics

Differential expression and regulatory networks.

Structural bioinformatics

Protein modeling and molecular docking.

Machine learning in genomics

Variant classification and multi-omics integration.
